<h2 align="center"><strong>About</strong></h2>

Catstle Blood is a 2D arcade game developed with Pygame. The game has 1 level and the level ends with a timeout event. It can be played coop (2 players).

---

### 📖 Title meaning
The name **Catstle Blood** comes from the idea of a **“cat castle”** — a feline fortress where cats defend against waves of enemies. The word *Catstle* is a playful fusion of **Cat** + **Castle**, highlighting the theme of heroic cats in battle.
